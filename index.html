Idle
Tap the mic and put the phone near the people talking.
English subtitles appear here, a few seconds behind the speakers.

This app only listens. It never speaks or replies.

Settings

Providers

You can mix providers, e.g. xAI for listening and OpenAI for translation.

API keys

🔒 Your keys are stored only on this device, in this browser (localStorage). Audio and text go straight from this phone to the provider you choose, never through any other server.

Please set a monthly spending limit on your provider account (OpenAI: platform.openai.com → Settings → Limits; xAI: console.x.ai → Billing), so a lost phone or a bug can never cost much.

Languages

If you know everyone is speaking one language, pick it. It's more accurate.

Listening

Shorter = faster subtitles but more broken sentences. Chunks are cut at a pause when possible. 5 s is a good start.
Quiet chunks aren't sent, which saves money. The yellow mark on the level bar shows the cut-off.
Advanced: model names

About

Listen-only: this app never plays audio and never answers anyone. Each few seconds of sound is turned into text by the speech-to-text service, then translated. Session usage: 0.0 min of audio sent.
